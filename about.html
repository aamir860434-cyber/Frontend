About you: your data on ConnectChat

📦 What we keep

  • Account: your name, username, email address and a salted hash of your password (we never store the password itself and cannot read it).
  • Profile: bio, profile photo, and your “show online status / last seen” choice.
  • Messages: message ciphertext for new text messages, older message text that may be plaintext, attachment references, replies, times and delivery/read status.
  • Calls: who called whom, voice or video, start time, duration and result (answered / missed / declined). The audio and video of a call are not recorded or stored; they go directly between the two devices (or through a relay server if needed).
  • Contacts: people you add and people you block. Phone contact import and reporting are currently disabled.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, a browser push address so we can notify you. You can switch it off any time.
  • Short-lived items: OTP codes and password-reset links expire in 10-30 minutes; rate-limit counters expire automatically.

🛡️ How your data is protected

  • Connections are protected by HTTPS when the service is deployed behind a correctly configured TLS endpoint.
  • Passwords are hashed with scrypt. Login sessions use secure HttpOnly cookies, and changing your password signs out old sessions.
  • Signup needs a verified email (OTP), one email can have only one account, and rate limits slow down bots and password guessing; captcha is enabled when the operator configures it.
  • New text messages are encrypted on the sender device and decrypted on the recipient device. The current prototype uses first-use key pinning (TOFU), not a fully authenticated Signal-style protocol. A first-contact man-in-the-middle attack is not prevented. Older messages may remain plaintext in the database.
  • Blocked users cannot message or call you. Blocking is available in chat settings.
  • Security headers and strict origin checks protect the site from hijacking.

Honest limits: only newly sent text messages between E2EE-enabled accounts are encrypted at the application layer. Images, videos, voice notes, documents, profile photos and metadata are not end-to-end encrypted and can be accessed by authorized service/database operators. This cryptographic design has not received an independent audit and must not be treated as equivalent to Signal. Verify a contact key through a separate trusted channel; first-contact MITM protection is not yet provided.

✅ Your controls

  • Settings → Clear all chats removes your chat history from your view.
  • Settings → Delete my account deletes your account and associated application records and removes your access from other users’ media. Encrypted/offsite backups may remain until their configured retention period ends.
  • Settings → Show activity status hides your online status and last seen.
